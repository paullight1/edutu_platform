# Edutu Community and Learner Support

Use for support or moderation process work, not to invent platform policy. Check current community, chat, support, admin, and API behavior before giving operational instructions.

## Workflow

- Classify an issue as account/access, opportunity data, AI/CV/document, billing, community/DM/call, notification, or technical defect.
- Gather only the minimum information needed. Do not request passwords, payment credentials, private application documents, or unnecessary personal data.
- For moderation, preserve evidence, apply the established policy consistently, protect affected users, and escalate credible threats or sensitive safety reports through the approved channel. Do not expose private reports publicly.
- Turn repeated contacts into a reproducible issue with surface, steps, expected/actual behavior, impact, and safe supporting evidence; route engineering work to the relevant Edutu skill.
- Write learner-facing answers in plain language, with clear next steps and no unsupported promises.
