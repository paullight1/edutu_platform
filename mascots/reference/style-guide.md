# Edutu Mascot Visual DNA

## Source anchor

This library is designed to sit beside the current Edutu illustration assets in `edutu-web-app/public/illustrations/` and uses the same product-brand direction rather than introducing an unrelated cartoon brand. The existing illustration family remains the visual reference for future refinements.

## Character language

- Friendly youth-first characters with a **large expressive head and compact body** so faces remain legible in small social posts.
- Rounded geometry, smooth silhouettes, soft dimensional shading and a subtle grounded shadow.
- Warm, optimistic expressions: capable and ambitious rather than childish.
- African-youth representation is deliberate: a broad range of deeper-to-lighter skin tones and natural hair systems including coils, puffs, curls, locs, braids, fades, twists and buns.
- Props are simplified into bold shapes so the subject still reads at carousel-thumbnail size.

## Edutu color system

Primary character accents use deep forest/green with mint/lime/yellow. Secondary accents may use sky, lavender, rose and orange, but green/yellow should remain the strongest visual cue across a campaign.

Core swatches:

- Forest `#173F35`
- Edutu green `#0A8F72`
- Mint `#42D6AD`
- Lime `#A6E66C`
- Yellow `#F6C748`
- Ink `#17312B`
- Cream `#FFF9EE`

## What may vary

Skin tone, hair, clothing color, props, action, emotion, accessories and campaign context.

## What should stay stable

Rounded visual language, friendly facial construction, compact proportions, clean silhouette, soft depth, brand accents and transparent-background master format.

## Social-media rules

1. Prefer one strong mascot per slide; avoid visual clutter.
2. Leave negative space on the side the mascot is facing for headlines/CTAs.
3. Use PNG for publishing; retain SVG for editing and scaling.
4. Do not stretch, recolor skin, add photorealistic limbs/faces, or mix anime/flat-vector/photoreal styles within one campaign.
5. When creating a new mascot, add it to the generator configuration and regenerate the contact sheet/packs.
